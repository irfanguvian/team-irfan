# Task 1 — bulk discount

## Goal

A cart line qualifies for the 5% bulk discount at **ten units or more**.

## Files in scope

`discount.ts`, `discount.test.ts`

## Acceptance criteria

- Below 10 units: full price.
- **At exactly 10 units: the discount applies.**
- Above 10 units: the discount applies.

## Out of scope

Currency formatting, tax, multi-line carts.
